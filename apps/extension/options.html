Choose whether the extension collects in the background and how often it checks your provider pages.

Providers

Only enabled providers are read. Enabling a provider lets the extension open and reload that provider's page during collection; disabling one stops its collection and removes its stored readings.

Manual collection and the individual card refresh buttons remain available when automatic updates are paused.
