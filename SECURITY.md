# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability or exposed
credential. Use [GitHub's private security-advisory form](https://github.com/tonyperkins/ai-capacity-monitor/security/advisories/new)
and include reproduction steps, affected version, and any safe proof of impact.

We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 business days and provide a status
update within 14 days. Please do not access other users' accounts or publish
details until a fix is available and coordinated.

## Scope

Reports are especially useful for unintended provider-page access, disclosure
of stored secrets or snapshots, unsafe publishing behavior, and packaging or
update-integrity defects. Provider account, billing, or service availability
issues belong with the relevant provider unless Capacity Monitor caused them.
